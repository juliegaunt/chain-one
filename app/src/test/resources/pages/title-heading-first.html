Materials:

Worsted weight yarn, about 90 yds

5mm Hook

Instructions

Make 2.

Cuff

1. Ch8. Hdc in 2nd ch from hook and each ch across. Turn. – 7 st

2. Ch1. Hdc in BLO of each st across. Turn. – 7 st

Repeat until the cuff fits around your wrist.

Hand

1. Ch1. Work 30 sc evenly along the cuff edge. Ss to 1st sc. – 30 st

2. Ch3. Dc in next st and each st around. Ss to top of ch3. – 30 st
