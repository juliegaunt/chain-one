Easy Market Bag

Welcome back to the blog! Today I'm sharing a stretchy market bag that holds a surprising amount of produce.

Materials

  • DK weight cotton, about 200 yards
  • 4.0mm crochet hook
  • Scissors

Gauge:

Not important for this project.

Pattern

Round 1: Make a magic ring, 8 sc into the ring.
Round 2: 2 sc in each st around. (16 sc)

Round 3: *Ch 5, skip 1 st, sc in next* around.

  1. Fasten off.
  2. Weave in ends.
